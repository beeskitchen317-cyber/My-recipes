A little about Bee's Kitchen

A collection made from memories, family, and a love of cooking.

🌾 🥄 🧈

Welcome to my kitchen.

I've been cooking and helping in the kitchen for as long as I can remember. My grandfather and father worked in a restaurant together, and my nana taught me recipes and welcomed me into the kitchen when I was a kid.

Over the years, I've scribbled recipes on notebook paper and kept them together in a binder. Bee's Kitchen is a way to bring those recipes into one place, keep the stories behind them alive, and share a little taste of home with others.
